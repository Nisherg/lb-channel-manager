Privacy Policy

LB Channel Manager — effective July 17, 2026

LB Channel Manager ("the tool") is a private, internal application used by our video production studio to manage YouTube channels that we own or operate under authorized manager access. This policy describes what data the tool accesses, how it is used and stored, and how you can revoke access.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to access and manage authorized YouTube channels. By connecting a channel to the tool, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Data we access and store

  • OAuth credentials. When a channel owner or manager authorizes the tool, Google issues OAuth tokens. These tokens are stored on equipment controlled by our studio and are used solely to perform the channel management actions listed below.
  • Channel and video data. Video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags), thumbnails, playlist information, and channel analytics data (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) for authorized channels.

The tool does not access viewers' personal data, does not collect data about visitors of any website, and does not use cookies or advertising identifiers.

3. How the data is used

Accessed data is used exclusively to manage the authorized channels: updating video metadata, uploading thumbnails and videos, managing playlists, and preparing channel performance reports for the channel owner. Analytics data may be aggregated into reports shared only with the respective channel's owner.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share stored data or OAuth credentials with any third parties. Data for one client's channel is never shared with another client. Data is transferred only between the tool and Google/YouTube APIs over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Data storage and retention

OAuth tokens and cached channel data are stored on studio-controlled equipment with access restricted to our team. Tokens are retained while the channel management engagement is active and are deleted when the engagement ends or when access is revoked, whichever comes first.

6. Revoking access

A channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time via Google security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately invalidates the stored tokens. You may also contact us to have stored tokens and cached data deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

8. Contact
