LB Channel Manager

Internal YouTube channel management tool

LB Channel Manager is a private, internal software tool used by our video production studio to manage YouTube channels that we own or operate on behalf of our clients under authorized manager access.

What the tool does

The tool uses YouTube API Services to perform routine channel management tasks for authorized channels:

  • Updating video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, category)
  • Uploading and setting video thumbnails
  • Creating playlists and adding videos to them
  • Uploading and scheduling videos
  • Retrieving channel and video analytics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources) to prepare performance reports

Who uses it

The tool is not publicly available. It is used exclusively by our own team. Each managed channel is connected individually by its owner or authorized manager through Google's standard OAuth consent flow, and access can be revoked by the channel owner at any time.
